# DOCTRINE — THE CTA RULE (Corey, 2026-08-15)
Why Corey is good at business, in his own structure. Applies to every ask the stack makes.

## THE RULE
Every call to action must carry WHEN + WHERE + THE PATH. Otherwise it is not an ask.

- Vague ("catch up for coffee one day") = gets nothing
- Specific ("ok, Monday 9am") = gets a yes — commitment, not maybe
- Path they cannot ignore ("going to the movies, pick you up at 8") = the when,
  the where, AND the ride
- "When given yes/no, no is for sure" — ambiguity is the killer; a real offer
  leaves no room for a soft no
- "Compliment is to see you again — lacking when and where" — flattery without
  a time is not a CTA; it is a dead end
- "If not right now" — the CTA must land NOW or it never lands
